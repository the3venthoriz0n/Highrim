## The game

- **Skyrim Special Edition / Anniversary Edition** by **Bethesda Game Studios** (a ZeniMax Media company). This mod needs the base game, and Skyrim.esm is a master of its plugin.
- `Highrim.esp` contains records that are **cloned from Bethesda's `Skyrim.esm`** (potion and ability magic effects, the Skooma potion, a note, a spell, a cooking recipe, a keyword, image space modifiers and others; the list is in `tools/extract_templates.py`, the copies in `tools/vanilla_templates.json`). The vanilla data stays the property of Bethesda. `src/GameData.h` is generated from `Skyrim.esm` (jail and map-marker positions) by `tools/extract_game_data.py`.
