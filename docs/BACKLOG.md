4. **Rimerush: faster attack speed.** Add a weapon speed boost (the Weapon Speed Multiplier actor value, for example +20%) to the Rimerush potion and its stack levels, as a visible line with a description; pick the value after a test and keep it a named constant.
5. **Rimerush: remove most of the screen effects while the high lasts.** Keep at most a very light one (or none) during the 120 seconds; the comedown look and the stack levels stay as they are unless the owner says otherwise. Change the Rimerush screen effect constant in `tools/make_esp.py` / `src/Doses.cpp` (the vanilla QuickReflexesImod trigger and its strength curve) and update the README visuals table.
6. **Visible countdowns for addiction, comedown and withdrawal.** The player should see in Active Effects how long until the addiction ends (dependency below 10), and how long the comedown and the withdrawal last. Ideas, none verified in game:
   - **Comedown:** it is a timed spell (60 s), so the game's own Active Effects timer should already count it down; check that it shows.
   - **Addiction and withdrawal** are abilities with no timer. Options: (a) update the ability's name at runtime with the time left, for example "Addicted: Rimerush (about 9 days left)", refreshed about once a minute (needs a runtime name change on the SpellItem, unproven; the time left comes from the dependency level and the decay rate, in game days, and should account for the lived-time rule if that is added); (b) re-apply the ability as a timed spell whose duration is the remaining time converted to real seconds with the game's timescale; (c) a status page in the settings menu or a HUD line on demand ("Addiction status") that lists each drug's level, time to clear and withdrawal tier. Pick after a test of (a).
   - Withdrawal has no end time of its own: it ends with a dose or when the dependency falls below 10, so show the time left until the level falls below 10, and say so in the description.
